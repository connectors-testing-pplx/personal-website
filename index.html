About

Engineering with empathy for the next maintainer.

I'm a senior software engineer focused on the unglamorous work that keeps products fast and teams calm: distributed systems, observability, internal platforms, and the developer experience around them. Over the last decade I've led infrastructure that grew from a single region to a global footprint serving tens of millions of users.

My approach is simple — measure first, build small, ship often, and document relentlessly. I care about code that reads like it was written for a person, not a compiler. When I'm not on call, I write about systems design, mentor early-career engineers, and tinker with home-lab hardware.
